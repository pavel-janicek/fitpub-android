FP Client is an unofficial Android client for FitPub, a federated,
self-hosted fitness-tracking platform. Point the app at any FitPub instance
you or your community run and sign in with that account — the app talks
exclusively to the instance you configure.

Features:
* Home and federated timelines with pagination
* Create activities by uploading FIT/GPX/TCX files or entering them manually
* Track visualization on an OpenStreetMap map (osmdroid)
* Comments, reactions, follows and follow requests
* Analytics: weekly distance, training load, form and summaries
* Privacy zones, batch import, notification feed
* Guest mode for browsing a federated timeline without an account

No third-party tracking, no crash-reporting services, no proprietary
libraries: the app identifies itself to servers with the User-Agent
"FP-Client/<version>".
